# Hai Shower v0.1.20

## Fixes

These problems were found by watching two real showers with debug logging
on, through an ESPHome Bluetooth proxy, and comparing Home Assistant's history
with the shower's own records. The fixes are covered by unit tests but are not
yet confirmed on a real shower.

### Showers are no longer lost after a history sync times out

- **The shower that just ended is kept.** After a shower, the device sometimes
  sends its whole usage log but never the end-of-log marker. The sync then
  waited 120 s, timed out, and threw away every record it had received. "Last
  shower" went back to the previous shower, and the shower count and total
  water use didn't go up until the next sync. Records received before a
  timeout or disconnect are now kept.
- **History sync finishes in seconds instead of 2 minutes** when the end
  marker goes missing: 5 s with no new records after they have started
  arriving now counts as the end of the log. A dropped Bluetooth connection
  ends the sync at once instead of after the full timeout.
- Diagnostics report `last_history_sync_result: partial` when a sync hit an
  error but kept the records it received.

### Fewer false "unavailable" and "idle" blips

- **Brief Bluetooth drops no longer make the shower unavailable.** ESPHome
  proxies sometimes fail a single read (`status=133`) and drop the
  connection. Sensors used to go unavailable for 30–40 s each time, and
  "Status" flipped from running to idle and back mid-shower. For 90 s
  after the shower last answered, a failed poll, history sync or disconnect
  now keeps the sensors available with their last readings while the
  integration reconnects.
- **Flow rate no longer flickers to "unknown"** every 30 s during a shower.

### Shower status and current session

- **"Status" leaves "running" as soon as the water stops** and shows
  "syncing" while the shower's history downloads. It used to stay on
  "running" with the last flow rate for about 2 minutes.
- **"Current session duration" and "Current session water usage" now count up
  during the shower.** They used to show the previous shower's values until
  the shower ended. Water usage is estimated from the live flow rate, then
  replaced with the shower's own total when the shower reports its end.

## Upgrade Notes

- Requires Home Assistant 2026.6.0 or newer (unchanged).
- No configuration changes are required. Existing entities, settings and
  statistics are kept.
- If the shower really goes out of range, its sensors now show as unavailable
  up to about 2 minutes after it stops answering, instead of at the next poll.
